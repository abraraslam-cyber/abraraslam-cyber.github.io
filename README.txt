TOOLNEST — WEBSITE STARTER

This is a responsive static website starter for an English-language AI tools and technology blog.

IMPORTANT:
- This is a starter website, not an AdSense approval guarantee.
- Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in index.html canonical URL with your real domain.
- Replace placeholder email addresses with a real monitored email address.
- Review and customize Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, About and Contact pages to accurately describe your real site, tools, analytics, cookies, ads, and data practices.
- Add original, researched articles and genuine screenshots/experience. The sample articles are starter drafts and explicitly identify where first-hand evidence must be added.
- Do not publish claims that you tested a product unless you really tested it.
- Add a sitemap.xml and robots.txt and connect Google Search Console after deploying.
- Configure HTTPS/SSL, backups, updates, security, and cookie/consent controls appropriate to your audience and applicable law.
- To use with WordPress, recreate the design in a WordPress theme/page builder or use this as a design reference. These files are a static HTML site and are not a WordPress theme.

To preview locally, open index.html in a browser.
To publish, upload the folder contents to your hosting provider's public web directory.
