CREATOR TECH · STARTER GUIDE

A simple workflow for planning a YouTube video

A repeatable process can make video production easier to manage and improve consistency.

Beginner guide · Adapt this workflow to your channel

1. Define one viewer problem

Start with a specific question your intended viewer wants answered. A focused promise makes it easier to plan a useful video.

2. Research before scripting

Check official documentation and credible sources. Separate confirmed facts from opinions, and note dates when information may change.

3. Build a clear outline

Organize the video into a hook, the main explanation, a practical example, and a concise conclusion. Remove sections that do not support the viewer's question.

4. Prepare the visual plan

List the screen recordings, examples, diagrams, and on-screen text you need. Use assets you own or have permission to use.

5. Review before publishing

Check audio clarity, captions, factual accuracy, title accuracy, thumbnail readability, and any music or asset licenses.
